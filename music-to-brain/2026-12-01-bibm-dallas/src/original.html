4 The clinic

Music therapy helps, but modestly, briefly and with limited certainty

van der Steen 2025Aalbers 2017Geretsegger 2022Jespersen 2022Magee 2017
GRADE certainty from Cochrane reviews. Hollow: no effect or uncertain. Dashed: no pooled RCT evidence, narrative reviews only. No outcome reaches high. Long term effects (4 weeks after treatment) not shown in dementia.
4 The clinic

In dementia, music lifts mood and behavior, not memory or cognition

van der Steen et al., Music based therapeutic interventions for people with dementia, Cochrane 2025
This tempers Act 1: musical memory is preserved, but music therapy does not restore memory. The gap between mechanism and treatment is the theme of Act 4.
4 The clinic

Feelings improve. Measured function often does not

Jespersen 2022van der Steen 2025
Insomnia: three trials with objective measures showed no clear gain. Dementia: moderate certainty of no effect on agitation.
4 The clinic

Music lessons do not make children smarter, once the controls are fair

Sala and Gobet, Mem Cogn 2020reanalysis finds g about 0.23
54 studies. Near transfer to musical skill is real, far transfer to IQ or school grades is not supported. A reanalysis disputes the exclusion choices and finds a small positive effect. Use this as a methods lesson, not as an attack on music education.

4 The clinic

Across reviews, the same complaint: nobody can say exactly what the intervention was

5 Music you can experiment on

One study design this makes possible Planned

  1. Generate pairs that differ in one property only: predictability, style, tempo.
  2. Record EEG while people listen, including older adults and hearing aid users.
  3. Test whether the brain response tracks the property beyond acoustic features.
  4. Keep the generator settings as the reported dose.
Concrete enough that a biosignal lab could run it within a year. Emphasize the second step: the populations where clinical evidence is weakest.

Three open problems

  1. Intervene inside the model. Does alignment with the brain move with it?
  2. Tune the stimulus to the listener: hearing loss, cochlear implants, aging.
  3. Make the therapy reportable: a music dose that can be written down and repeated.
