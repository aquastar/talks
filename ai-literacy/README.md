# AI Literacy for CS Faculty

A ~25-minute AI literacy talk for the Fall 2026 CS faculty meeting, built with reveal.js. It is a tutorial on the core technology, not a discussion of whether AI is good or bad or of how teaching should change. It builds on the AI Committee's *AI Literacy Tutorial* draft.

**Part 1, How it works**, goes from next-token prediction up to agents:

- the nested circles of "AI", and a click-through timeline from 2012 to 2026
- what "GPT" stands for, tokenization, and autoregressive next-token sampling
- the hidden chat template, with pseudocode for a minimal harness
- how a chatbot is trained (pretraining, SFT, RLHF), and chain of thought
- tool calls in one context window, RAG, and the agent loop
- RLVR-trained reasoning models, MCP, and steering agents with markdown files (`CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, skills)
- what a working agent session looks like

**Part 2, Pace of change**, covers METR time horizons, context and cost trends, open-source models and harnesses, and the jagged frontier.

Handout: `glossary.md` (and `glossary.pdf`), a one-page glossary of the terms used in the talk.
